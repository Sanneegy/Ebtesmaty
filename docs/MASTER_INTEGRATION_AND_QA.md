## Verification

- Production compilation and type checking succeeded. The build still reports lint warnings about images and hook dependencies.
- 78 automated checks passed across 13 suites, including scheduling, queues, identity, clinical records, finances, management and localization.
- HTTP checks verified all eight fictional role destinations, rejected an external origin, and verified registration, email and phone login, logout, session persistence and the absence of demo access in ordinary login mode.
- Synthetic presentation audit: BR-A EGP 4,200 invoiced / 2,700 collected; BR-B 1,800 / 1,350; BR-C 1,200 / 600. These are presentation figures, not clinic performance.
- This is not a certification of every button, Arabic string, clinical or financial transition, accessibility criterion or production permission.
- Browser booking test, verification database: registered a new patient, signed in, selected a real future slot from the form, submitted it, and verified the stored request's patient ID, date and REQUESTED state. Requesting a time does not bypass reception confirmation.
- CRM API test, verification database: recorded contact and linked a confirmed booking, checked the persisted link, rejected a second link and rejected contact entry by the owner.
- Visual evidence: 28 workspace destinations × English/Arabic × desktop (1440×900)/mobile (390×844) = 112 viewport screenshots plus one isolated patient booking result. Both locale runs reported zero document-width overflow. Spot reviewed the Arabic patient dashboard and mobile receptionist board. These captures document the initial viewport; they do not cover every modal or every scroll position. Stored clinical descriptions and proper names can appear in their original language.

The queue prevents calling a second patient while the same dentist has an active called or in-consultation visit.
